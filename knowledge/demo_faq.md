# Billing and Account Management

## How does billing work?

- Monthly plans are billed on the same date each month
- Annual plans are billed upfront for the full year
- Payments are processed via Stripe (credit/debit card)
- Invoices are emailed automatically after each payment

## How do I cancel?

You can cancel at any time:
1. Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription
2. Follow the cancellation steps

Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
We do not offer prorated refunds for monthly plans that are partially used.
Annual plan refund policy: contact billing support within 14 days of renewal for assistance.

## Can I pause my subscription instead of cancelling?

Yes. If you need temporary time off without losing your customer records and settings:
- You can pause your subscription for up to 90 days from **Settings > Billing > Pause Account**.
- While paused, billing is suspended and all your customer data remains 100% safe and intact.
- You can unpause and resume your plan at any time with one click.

## Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes. You can change your plan at any time from Settings > Billing.
- Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated charge for the remainder of the billing period)
- Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period

# Frequently Asked Questions

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.
Enter your account email address.
You'll receive a password reset link valid for 1 hour.
Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.

## My account is locked. What do I do?

Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts.
Wait 15 minutes and try again, or use the password reset flow.
If you're still locked out, contact support with your account email address.

## Can I have multiple users on one account?

Yes. The number of users depends on your plan:
- Starter: up to 5 users
- Growth: up to 25 users
- Enterprise: unlimited users

Add users from Settings > Team Members > Invite User.

## I'm getting an error. What should I do?

1. Refresh the page and try again
2. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete or Cmd+Shift+Delete)
3. Try a different browser
4. Check our Status Page for any known outages
5. Contact support with the error message and your browser version

## What happens to my data if I cancel?

After cancellation, your data is retained for 30 days in case you want to reactivate.
After 30 days, data is permanently deleted from our systems.
You can export your data at any time from Settings > Data Export before cancelling.
